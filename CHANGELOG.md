# Release changes

## 1.1.0

Companion processed-data release: [10.5281/zenodo.24487407](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24487407).

- Preserved the original v1.0.0 harmonized screen, comparator and program inputs, historical source manifests and expected results. Two inherited whole-blood derivatives carry anonymous paired contrasts and aggregate summaries with individual-score columns excluded. The 20 biological pair contrasts retain their original values.
- Added the 23 November 2026 post-review detectability, regulator-class, strict-conditioning, LOEUF and gene-property sensitivity analyses. The release includes cohort counts, numerical warnings, fixed M0–M3 comparisons, coefficient and balance tables, matching feasibility and the historical model bridge.
- Added 245 frozen gene-level mechanism annotations, the 104-member scoped LoF-compatible sensitivity, source-native and interpreted knockout directions and experiment-level published candidate evidence.
- Added Zhu et al. (2026) summary data for 17 selected genes, condition-level coverage and source-reported ZMYND8 arrayed endpoints. The derived flow record counts three matched donor records and retains the six-donor bulk RNA arm as a distinct experiment.
- Added deterministic Figures 1–4 and Supplementary Figures S1–S6 renderers, final figure sources and equivalent UTF-8 source tables for Supplementary Tables S1–S12. The processed-data workflow reconstructs all ten figures without manuscript page underlays.
- Added a single relative-path workflow for release integrity, legacy and adjusted statistical comparisons, independent base-R profiles, Zhu-derived checks and figure reconstruction.
