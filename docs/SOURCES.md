# Sources and reuse

The extracted archive includes the per-component source inventory `data/manifests/COMPONENT_SOURCE_MAP.tsv`, the source rights table `data/manifests/sources/source_rights_access.tsv`, excluded source files in `data/manifests/sources/excluded_upstream_inputs.tsv` and official-source replay inputs in `data/manifests/EXTERNAL_REPLAY_INPUTS.tsv`. The ledgers distinguish source terms, released derivatives and separate acquisition. GEO deposits retain the applicable submitter terms. [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html)

## Screen datasets and patient context

- **Chen et al.** The human induced-Treg FOXP3 screen supplies harmonized MAGeCK summaries, assigned knockout directions and program and Perturb-icCITE-seq context. DDBJ E-GEAD-649 supplies the expression references. PMID 40140585. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08795-5) · [Correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09356-6) · [DDBJ E-GEAD-649](https://ddbj.nig.ac.jp/public/ddbj_database/gea/experiment/E-GEAD-000/E-GEAD-649/)
- **Umhoefer et al.** The targeted primary human Treg and Tconv screens supply separately retained FOXP3 readouts and assay comparisons. Exact gene and guide workbooks are external inputs. PMID 41237776. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2025.10.020) · [GEO GSE286470](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE286470)
- **Phenotype and patient context.** Shifrut T-cell proliferation, Loo mouse Treg with MGI one-to-one orthology, Moreno purified-Treg effects, Yan Treg-like annotation and paired GSE212645 whole-blood differences retain their separate endpoints. The whole-blood release uses published anonymous pair-level differences. [Shifrut](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.10.024) · [Loo](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32640256/) · [Moreno](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41703554/) · [Yan](https://doi.org/10.1002/ctm2.70552) · [GSE212645](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE212645)

## Expression, annotations and perturbation evidence

- **Human and mouse atlases.** Monaco et al. human immune-cell expression and Yoshida et al. and the Immunological Genome Project mouse immune-cell expression retain their published processed inputs, reference mappings and respective TPM and TMM CPM units. [Monaco, PMID 30726743](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.01.041) · [GSE107011](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE107011) · [ImmGen, PMID 30686579](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.036) · [GSE109125](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE109125)
- **Mouse conditional knockouts.** Konopacki et al. Foxp1 (PMID 30643266), Christensen et al. Sin3a (PMID 39156895) and Ren et al. Foxp1 (PMID 31125332) supply the published processed count inputs and official study metadata. [Konopacki](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-018-0291-z) · [GSE121279](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE121279) · [Christensen](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1444937) · [GSE263830](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263830) · [Ren](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000270) · [GSE121251](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE121251)
- **Gene sets.** Hill et al. Treg-identity sets use human C7 ImmuneSigDB 2025.1.Hs. Mouse Hallmark comparator programs use MSigDB 2025.1.Mm. Selected or mapped sets retain MSigDB acknowledgement and its terms and exceptions. [Hill, PMID 18024188](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2007.09.010) · [Liberzon, PMID 26771021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004) · [MSigDB terms](https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb_license_terms.jsp)
- **Gene properties and classification.** The frozen SFARI 2026 Q2 score-1 labels and approved identities are supported by HGNC. Study fields include G2P mechanism, gnomAD v2.1.1 LOEUF, TF and EpiFactors memberships, selected HPA aggregate expression and the GSE90600 healthy CD4 reference. Gene models use GENCODE v26 and vM25, with MGI orthology where recorded. Selected or derived fields retain provider attribution. [SFARI](https://gene.sfari.org/tools/) · [HGNC](https://hgnc.genenames.org/download/) · [G2P](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gene2phenotype/download) · [gnomAD](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/downloads#v2-constraint) · [TF catalogue](https://humantfs.ccbr.utoronto.ca/) · [EpiFactors](https://epifactors.autosome.org/) · [HPA](https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/download) · [GSE90600](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE90600) · [GENCODE](https://www.gencodegenes.org/) · [MGI](https://www.informatics.jax.org/mgihome/other/copyright.shtml)
- **Published functional evidence.** The package contains attributed experiment and endpoint summaries for Chen, Umhoefer, Schumann, Kolb and Zhu with assay-specific biological units. The Zhu accession ledger is `data/experimental_evidence/zhu_perturbation/zhu_source_files.tsv`. The catalogue lists MIT for its processed release while the article is CC BY-NC 4.0. The underlying flow CSV is outside the catalogue list. The data package carries aggregate cited endpoints and coverage. [Schumann](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-020-0784-4) · [Kolb](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-026-00812-6) · [Zhu](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.002) · [Zhu processed dataset](https://virtualcellmodels.cziscience.com/dataset/genome-scale-tcell-perturb-seq)
