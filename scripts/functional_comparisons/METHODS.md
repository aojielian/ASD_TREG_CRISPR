## A. Directional exact enrichment in the 104 LoF-compatible genes

Use final frozen score-1 and LoF flags. Other score-1 genes are omitted from the case/control comparison by the original strict conditioning rule. Leave ASD-named and other subgroups descriptive; no alternate variant interpretation. Use measured CHEN and UMHOEFER_TREG rows with primary CPM ≥1 detectability and the released strict-joint covariate requirements and strata: four regulator classes, Chen targeted-library membership, average-rank tertiles of primary log expression and log10 span, and guide groups 1–3/4/≥5. Tertiles and strata must be reproduced using the released population ordering, never recomputed only on cases. LOEUF does not enter these exact strata. Check every frozen LOF_COMPATIBLE CPM 1 strict-joint N/H/A/K and ALL_104_LOF scope counts before directional inference; exclude unmeasured genes. Confirm all author hits and 51 candidate directions against frozen screen-specific mapping. An operational support hit corresponds to a reduction after KO, a restraint hit to an increase. Opposite-direction hits are outcome 0 for each direction. Ambiguous directions are explicitly counted and do not become positives arbitrarily.

Four upper-tail hypergeometric-convolution tests, two screens × two directions, use the exact per-stratum N/H/A/K. Report observed and expected hits, O/E, variance, and equal-tailed central 95% null count interval defined by CDF quantiles .025 and .975. Use one Holm family of four; non-informative variance-zero tests have displayed NA P with a value of one solely for adjustment. A distinct family of four upper-tail Fisher tests uses the exact same complete-case population and conditional-MLE OR with a two-sided equal-tailed exact 95% confidence interval. Full measured and detected denominators and both directions are descriptive. Record covariate representation, comparator and endpoint-informative case coverage; compare the original 80% of measured cases / 70% of strict cases coverage diagnostic. These thresholds qualify scope and do not change the tests.

## B. Property-conditioned FOXP3 versus TCR-stimulated proliferation

Rebuild D from the two author hit flags on the frozen common measured universe: +1 Chen-only, −1 Shifrut-only, 0 both/neither. The original unconditioned exact test samples the 242 ASD labels from that universe. Join exactly once to the measured Chen rows and retain cases and controls with four-level class, known targeted-library membership, finite frozen healthy-CD4 mean logCPM and finite LOEUF v2.1.1. Report overlapping missingness and final eligibility by group. Healthy-CD4 expression is a cross-assay proxy. Compare the original full universe, unadjusted complete cases and the conditioned complete cases. Fixed strata have class × library membership × healthy-CD4 tertile × LOEUF tertile. Both tertiles use min(3, ceil(3 × average rank/n)) across all complete cases with ties kept together. All complete-case strata, including deterministic strata, contribute to the null. Record assignment, ranks/thresholds, N/A and D margins. Perform one upper-tail exact multivariate-hypergeometric test based on signed-score convolution. Report observed S, E[S], difference, variance, central 95% null count interval, separate expected/observed ASD FOXP3-only and TCR-only counts and support metrics. Independently verify exact signed PMF (toy exhaustive enumeration plus independently computed full/complete-case/stratified probabilities). Preserve original coverage benchmarks of 80% of the 242 measured cases and 70% of complete-case cases in informative strata as diagnostics, with explicit continuous balance and positivity. Use |SMD| >0.10 as a residual-imbalance flag, not a selection rule.
