# Umhoefer screen data and statistical fields

The analyzed GEO deposition is GSE286470, with the gene and guide spreadsheets and SOFT under the external `GSE286470` directory listed in `inputs.tsv`. The accepted workbook snapshots are specified by SHA-256. The 2026-10-07 SOFT lists PMID 41237776 for the experiment and PMID 42664972 for a distinct linked study. The latter supplies no measurements in this analysis. The two sorted bins are technical outcomes within each donor/context, represented here by a GSM for each bin. Donor labels recur across Treg and Tconv without a cross-context person key.

[Umhoefer et al., Immunity, PMID 41237776](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2025.10.020) · [GEO GSE286470](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE286470) · [Linked Zhu et al. study, PMID 42664972](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42664972/)

## Gene results

- `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_universe.tsv.gz` is the complete shared gene population. `gene` and `hgnc_id` are frozen canonical identities, `asd` is a frozen SFARI score-1 indicator, `source_row` fields refer to physical one-based Excel rows. The original MAGeCK effect and FDR fields are retained by Treg/Tconv prefix. A positive `signed_foxp3_bin_lfc` describes enrichment in the higher FOXP3 sort bin after knockout, reversing the sign of this source workbook's native selection LFC. Raw magnitudes summarize differential guide representation. `*_hit` is the union of the two author FDR cutoffs. `*_effect_sign` also records the sign of measured non-hits for descriptive use. `category` reflects author hits in both screened contexts and is defined only for jointly evaluable genes. `delta_*_rank` compares within-context scaled ranks (Treg minus Tconv). Frozen FOXP3–TCR attribute data include binary TF and EpiFactors indicators, their joint class, targeted-library membership, healthy-CD4 mean logCPM (`expression`) and gnomAD LOEUF v2.1.1 (`LOEUF`). `gene_span_bp` and each context's `guide_n` are retained as audit quantities. The healthy-CD4 expression is an external proxy. Complete cases are specified by `complete_case`; `adjustment_exclusions` gives explicit reasons. The two average-rank tertiles and `stratum` refer to the FOXP3–TCR matching specification.
- `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_counts.tsv` includes shared hit categories, same/opposite direction within both-context hits, and the separate measured and shared backgrounds. `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_primary.tsv` gives the primary 2×2 test and descriptive individual screen tests. `OR` and `CI_low/CI_high` use the conditional maximum-likelihood odds ratio and equal-tail exact 95% interval; `sample_OR` is the raw cross-product ratio. `P` is two-sided and `upper_tail_P` is a descriptive calculation for legacy comparison. `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_sensitivity.tsv` records the fixed top-fraction analyses, actual fractions accounting for ties and three-test BH-adjusted P. Its four Fisher count fields compare top-ranked Treg-only and top-ranked Tconv-only genes.
- `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_continuous.tsv` reports ASD-minus-other mean and median differences of Δrank within U, with quartiles and percentile bootstrap 95% CIs. `P` for the mean is the two-sided equal-size label permutation P with a +1 correction, and `mc_se` is its binomial Monte Carlo error. The bootstrap intervals and permutation P values come from separate procedures. The signed median is descriptive and receives no P. The null distribution table preserves 100,000 actual permuted means for each mean absolute-rank and signed-rank result, each unadjusted or property-conditioned.
- `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_adjusted.tsv` records three binary estimands and two continuous estimands. The discordant exact fixed-margin count has a central 95% null interval (`null_q025/null_q975`), with neither a conditional OR nor an effect CI. The Firth sensitivities use the same discordant complete cases and report penalized profile likelihood CIs and P, plus convergence and covariate diagnostics. `dropped_constant_terms` records library membership in the full model because the entire tested set is from the library. For continuous adjusted ranks the estimand is the ASD-count-weighted within-stratum difference using strata with both label groups. `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_match_balance.tsv` and `data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/treg_tconv_conditioning_strata.tsv` document complete-case coverage, strata, exclusions and residual SMD. Conditional support for binary inference requires both label groups and both outcomes. Continuous inference requires both label groups.
- `data/experimental_evidence/candidate_integration/14_gene_function_profile.tsv` contains the fixed panel with separate measurement and hit indicators, exact source fields and per-screen interpretations. `NA` can indicate an unavailable value, unmeasured gene or inapplicable statistic. Screen status fields specify why. Shifrut selection LFCs use that study's native TCR-stimulated proliferation assay and have no FOXP3-bin unit. [Shifrut et al., Cell, PMID 30449619](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30449619/)

## Guide and input audit

`data/functional_comparisons/treg_tconv/results/umhoefer_guide_audit.tsv` reports entire-panel guide availability and descriptive direction for measured candidates. The aggregate and candidate guide-source-row tables contain no guide sequences. The workbook provides a normalized count vector for each guide in each bin context, with unlabelled vector positions and a pooled effect. The SOFT sample metadata establish local within-context donor groups. The input gives no link from vector position to named donor and no published per-donor gene effect. One non-panel human gene, BMAL2 (source label ARNTL2), has three counted guides in both gene rows and four guide workbook rows. This workflow preserves the valid frozen and source gene-level result.
